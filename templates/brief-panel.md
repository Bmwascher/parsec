# Panel insert

How a panel is asked. The host gives, in part 2: the question; the options; the constraints; Brandon's exact words; the evidence as file paths, never a summary. Then, unless the panel is blind, its own recommendation with its reasons, the strongest argument against it, and what it is unsure of.

Each lane answers in this order:

1. Is there a better option NOT on the list? Always answered; "none" needs a reason.
2. Its ranking of the options.
3. Whether it agrees with the host, and on what evidence.
4. What it could not verify.

The verdict word means: PASS, the host's recommendation stands; FIX, another option is better; ESCALATE, the evidence cannot settle it.

In a blind panel (Brandon's request) the lane ends with `VERDICT: BLIND` instead of a judgment word, and its ranking is its answer.
